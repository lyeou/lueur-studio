06 / Our story

For the pleasure
of making.

We believe the things we make can bring a little more joy to the everyday.

Lueur Studio begins with a simple idea: making should feel welcoming. A beautiful, useful object matters — and so does the afternoon spent creating it.

Design is the entry point. Experience is the product. Culture is the depth.

Start with fabric. Follow your curiosity.
